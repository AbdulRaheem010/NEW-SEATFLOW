FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything organizers ask us before their first event.

Guests & privacy

No. Guests scan your QR code and it opens directly in their phone’s browser — nothing to download or sign up for.

No. Public lookup returns only the matching guest’s seating result. A full guest directory is not exposed.

SeatFlow asks for one more identifying detail to tell them apart before showing a result.

Tablemate visibility is not currently configurable from the event builder.

Setup & changes

Add guests one at a time from the event dashboard. A bulk-import workflow is not currently enabled.

The change is live the moment you save it. Your printed QR code and sign never need to be reprinted.

The current Seating Builder includes Auto Arrange for a first-pass layout. The AI optimizer and rule-based recommendation workflow are not enabled yet.

You can explore the public demo without registering. Creating and managing a real event requires an account.

Pricing & plans

Not yet. The current SeatFlow build does not process payments or enforce paid subscription plans.

You can create events, manage guests, create tables, arrange seating, publish QR guest lookup and check guests in.
