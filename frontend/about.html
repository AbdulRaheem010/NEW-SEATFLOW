About SeatFlow

Digital seating without the seating-chart headache.

SeatFlow brings event guests, tables, seating assignments and QR-based lookup into one organizer workflow.

The product

One place to plan, publish and manage seating.

Organizers can create events, manage guest lists, build tables, assign seats, publish a public guest page and check guests in from the same account.

What SeatFlow focuses on

Simple for guests. Practical for organizers.

Less paper

Use a QR code and public event page instead of relying on a static printed seating board.

Fewer moving parts

Keep guest records, tables, seating assignments and check-in in one system.

Mobile access

Guests can look up their seating information from a phone without installing an app.
