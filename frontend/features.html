Features

Everything you need to seat a room, in one place.

From the first guest you add to the last check-in at the door — SeatFlow covers the whole workflow.

Seating

Build the arrangement visually.

◫

Drag-and-drop tables

Place round, square and rectangular tables on a canvas, then resize and rotate them to match your floor.

☰

Guest pool

Search, filter by group or VIP status, and drag guests straight onto a table.

↺

Undo, redo & auto-arrange

Experiment freely — every change can be undone, and auto-arrange handles the first pass for you.

✦

AI seating optimizer

Set rules like “keep families together” or “separate conflicting guests,” then review every suggested change before applying it.

⚑

Smart seating rules

Define must-sit-together, must-not-sit-together, and VIP placement rules that carry across every revision.

⌨

Accessible assignment

An accessible “assign guest → select table” flow works fully without drag-and-drop.

Guest data

Bring your list in, keep it clean.

▤

CSV & Excel import

Upload your file, map the columns, preview the results, and see exactly what’s valid before you import.

⚠

Duplicate & error detection

SeatFlow flags duplicate and invalid rows during import instead of after the fact.

⬇

Export anytime

Pull your guest list, seating assignments and check-in status back out whenever you need to.

Guest experience

What your guest actually sees.

◎

QR lookup

Scan once, search a name, see a table — no app required.

◐

Privacy mode

Choose whether tablemates are shown, or keep each guest’s result private.

▣

Schedule & menu

Give guests the run of show and tonight’s menu right from their result page.

⛶

Floorplan

An interactive, zoomable venue floorplan guests can explore from their seat lookup.

◷

Countdown

A live countdown to doors opening, right on the guest landing page.

◍

Photo sharing

Guests upload their own photos to a shared gallery you can moderate.

✓

Check-in

A live check-in count and searchable guest list for your door staff.

⇄

Real-time updates

Every change you make is reflected the next time a guest looks up their name.
