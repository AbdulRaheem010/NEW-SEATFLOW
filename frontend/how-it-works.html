How it works

From spreadsheet to seated in four steps.

No app for guests to install, no printed board to replace on the day. Just a name search and a table number.

Type guests in one by one, or import your existing spreadsheet. Map the columns once and SeatFlow does the rest — flagging duplicates and errors before anything is saved.
